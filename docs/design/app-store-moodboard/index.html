04 — VOICE

Reassure.
Don’t overclaim.

Say “Review every document” and “In-person notarization.” Avoid “legally guaranteed,” “instant notarization,” “blockchain secured,” or implying notary fees are included.

04 / FROM MOODBOARD TO SUBMISSION

The capture brief.

One fictional member, one coherent demo package, one current build. Capture the experience as it actually ships, then wrap it in the visual system above. No client screenshots, real signatures, addresses or identifiers.

No.MessageActual screen to captureReadiness / art-direction note

Export correctly.

Use a 1320 × 2868 px portrait master for the accepted 6.9-inch slot. Apple accepts 1–10 screenshots in JPEG/JPG/PNG, without alpha. Recheck the submitted binary’s supported devices; the current project targets iPhone, but iPad support would require its own assets.

Apple screenshot specifications ↗

Show the shipped app.

Use real app-in-use captures, not a splash/login-only sequence. Overlays may explain the experience, but shouldn’t imply unavailable features. Use fictional data and disclose paid requirements accurately. The proposed opening should include “Membership required. Notary fees separate.”

Apple accurate-metadata guidance ↗

Separate art from release gates.

Confirm signature saving, the full trust signing package and the completed-file/verification flow before capture. Membership checkout and notifications also need to work, even if they aren’t the marketing hero.

Make a reviewer-access plan for the production IP restriction. Screenshots do not resolve reviewer access, app behavior or payment-review requirements.

My recommendation: approve this green / black / white direction, then produce frames 01–03 with real screens first. Review them at thumbnail size before building the complete set. A later alternate opening can test a more explicit “Prepare. Sign. Notarize.” against “Make it official.”—a hypothesis to test, not a promised conversion lift.
