**Step 1 – Trust Name**

DARCi can register all revocable Trusts, which can be amended or revoked by the individual or individuals who created them. Most Trusts are revocable, and the legal presumption is that a Trust is revocable unless it specifies otherwise. However, if the individual(s) who created the Trust has/have lost capacity, the Trust may become irrevocable due to the lack of capacity of the persons who made it.

**Step 2 – Trustmaker(s)**

A trustmaker is the individual who created the Trust and owns the assets of the Trust. The Trust document may refer to them as a Grantor, Settlor, Trustor, or Trustmaker. These titles all refer to the individual or individuals who own the assets and created the Trust to hold them. (\*\*\*This differs from the Trustee; we will discuss that role next.)

Enter the exact name of the Trustmaker as it appears in the Trust and/or on Trust assets. It may or may not include a middle initial, middle name, or nickname.

Check this box if the Trustee (manager) is acting as the Trustee. Until resignation, incapacity, removal, or death, the Trustee is generally also the acting Trustee.

Sometimes, two or more individuals create a joint Trust. This is often a married couple, siblings, friends, relatives, business partners, etc. If more than one individual created the Trust and owns the assets, add all of them.

Taxpayer Identification Number If the Trust is joint, typically, the T ax ID of one of the Trustmakers is the primary and shows as primary on Federal and State tax returns. Please select which T rustmaker’s T ax ID is primary.

**Step 3 – Trustee**

The Trustee is the manager of the Trust assets. While the Trustmakers are alive, capable, and willing to act, they are almost always the Trustees. However, due to resignation, incapacity, removal, or death of the Trustmaker, the acting Trustee could be the nominated Successor.

**Step 5 – T rustee Powers & Signing Authority**

The powers of the T rustee are listed in the T rust, generally under the “Trustee Powers” section. This can be found in your Certification of T rust or Abstract of T rust. Attornies, books, and software may use different words for the same things. Instead of asking you to interpret legalese or guess, DARCi registration asks you to answer these questions anew. It will include your answers in your Registration Amendment so that they become new terms of the T rust.  
If there is more than one T rustee, the T rust might state that the signature of either T rustee  
is sufficient to bind the T rust. However, if the T rust does not state anything about this, the  
default rule usually requires the signatures of all acting T rustees (not successor T rustees).

**Step 6 – T rust Revocation & Trustee Incapacity Standard**

If more than one T rustmaker created the T rust, the T rust will specify how it may be revoked. For example, if a married couple created the T rust, the T rust often states that each Trustmaker can revoke the T rust as to their property only, but both T rustmakers acting jointly can revoke the entire Trust.

To protect Trustees, T rusts often specify how an acting Trustee’s incapacity (and removal) is to be determined. Sometimes, it is upon evaluation by one or two licensed, treating physicians not related by blood or marriage. However, if the Trust does not state anything about this, you can make your preferred selection here and incorporate this term into your Trust.

**Step 7 – Upload Documents**

Upload all the documents that constitute your T rust terms. The originating document will  
be either a T rust Agreement or Declaration of T rust; specify which here and enter the date  
it was signed.

Add your documents one at a time until all T rust terms are captured. This will include  
Schedules of Assets, Amendments, Affidavits, Incapacity Letters, etc.

**Step 8 – illuminotarize to Finalize the Registration**

Print and save your DARCi Record Number. Contact an illuminotary to finalize your trust registration on the DARCi Registry and make it a legally binding document. You can find a list of illuminotaries on our website. The illuminotary might ask you for your DARCi Registration number when you make your appointment so they can have your documents ready when you meet.
