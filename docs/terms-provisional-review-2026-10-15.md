### 10. Hashes and public verification

DARCi records a SHA-256 hash of the exact final PDF bytes. Comparing another
file's SHA-256 value with that recorded value can show whether the files are
byte-for-byte identical. A different value means the bytes differ; it does not
identify the change, prove fraud or decide which version is legally effective.
Printing, scanning, re-saving or changing PDF metadata may change the hash even
if the pages appear the same. Use the original downloaded file for comparison.

Hash-only verification is not blockchain anchoring, an external-ledger record,
an independent timestamp certification or a guarantee about the accuracy of the
document's contents or a signer's legal authority.

**Released final documents can be previewed through their public verification
link or IDN without signing in. Anyone who obtains that link or identifier may
view and save the exposed final PDFs and information printed in them.** Do not
treat a public verification link as confidential or assume a browser preview
prevents downloading. Drafts, held packages and protected identity records are
not included in that public preview. Share documents only with the rights and
consents needed for their contents and intended exposure.
