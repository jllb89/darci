## 1. Membership and renewal — proposed customer wording

DARCi membership is for document owners. Notaries and invited signers do not pay
for those roles. All plans have the same product features. Notary fees are separate.

| Plan | Monthly allowance | Monthly payment | Annual payment, paid up front |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 3 document workflows | US$9.99 | US$99 |
| Plus | 25 document workflows | US$19.99 | US$199 |
| Unlimited | No monthly workflow quota | US$59.99 | US$599 |

Prices are before applicable taxes. Your subscription renews at the selected
monthly or annual cadence unless you cancel renewal. Annual payment does not
convert to monthly payment; Starter and Plus allowances still renew monthly.
Unused allowance does not roll over.

One submitted Trust package, standalone power of attorney, or uploaded-document
notarization counts as one workflow. Drafts, regeneration/retries, signatures,
additional PDFs in that package, notary steps and downloads do not count again.
Unlimited has no hidden numerical monthly quota; standard file, security and
processing limits still apply.

Same-cadence upgrades take effect after the required payment, with proration and
current monthly usage preserved. Downgrades and monthly/annual cadence changes
take effect at the existing paid-through date. Canceling renewal preserves access
through that date, including monthly allowance resets within a paid annual term.

**Before publication:** identify the contracting legal entity, postal/contact
details, effective date, applicable customer terms URL, accepted territories and
the approved renewal/consent disclosures. Existing pricing/default approval is
recorded in [the pricing decision](member-pricing-v2-review-proposal-2026-09-23.md).

## 2. Accepted work and held final packages — proposed notices

Before purchase / lapse:

> If your membership ends, a notary can still finish work they already accepted.
> A newly completed final package that has not yet been released will remain
> unavailable until your membership is reactivated. Packages already released
> remain accessible to their authorized users. Notary fees are separate.

At a held package:

> Your notary has completed this package. Reactivate your membership to access
> the final document with its acknowledgment and seal. Your existing signatures
> and document are preserved; reactivation does not regenerate the document.

Notary-facing notice:

> You can complete this accepted request even if the member’s membership ends.
> The member’s access to a newly completed final package may remain on hold until
> they reactivate their membership.

**Review needed:** whether this access restriction is acceptable for the offered
service/jurisdictions, required advance notices, emergency/support exceptions,
and whether invited parties require different access. Do not treat the implemented
hold as a legal determination. Public verification exposes status/hash only,
never a public PDF; currently held packages are not publicly discoverable.

## 3. Taxes — decisions for finance, not a tax policy already implemented

The approved commercial decision is **USD, before applicable taxes**. Tax-exclusive
Stripe Prices do not establish what tax applies or collect it automatically.

Finance/client must supply:

1. The selling legal entity and its business address.
2. Launch customer jurisdictions and any relevant tax registrations.
3. The approved treatment of membership separately from third-party notary fees.
4. Whether Stripe Tax or another approved collection mechanism will be used,
   and the product tax code, address collection and invoice disclosures it needs.
5. Who maintains registrations, filings and changes after launch.

Do not imply that no tax is due simply because collection is not yet configured.
The isolated operator payment is a controlled acceptance test, not permission
to launch unreviewed tax treatment to customers.

## 4. Refunds and disputes — proposed operational process, eligibility undecided

Support/reply-to: `lopezb.jl@gmail.com` (approved). Cancellation stops future
renewal; it is not an automatic refund of an earlier payment.

Proposed support process: identify the customer and invoice; record the request
and relevant service history; route it to the designated operator; communicate
the decision and expected processing timeline; retain an audit of any approved
refund or dispute response. Never request card details by email. Avoid automatic
document deletion or changes to signed evidence when handling a billing complaint.

**Client/counsel must decide before publication:** refund eligibility/window,
partial refunds, unused annual periods, duplicate payments, unsuccessful service,
exceptions/mandatory rights, response target, dispute owner and evidence process.
No blanket “no refunds” policy, guaranteed refund, or automatic dispute action
has been approved. Refunds require separate operational authorization.
